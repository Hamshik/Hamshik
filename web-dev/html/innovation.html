Pilikula Regional Science Centre

🧪 Pilikula Regional Science Centre – Overview:
The Pilikula Regional Science Centre (PRSC), located in Mangaluru, Karnataka, is a vibrant hub for science education and exploration. Jointly established by the Department of Science & Technology, Government of Karnataka, and the National Council of Science Museums, it aims to promote scientific curiosity among students and the general public.

🌟 Key Features

  • Interactive Exhibits: Fun science, biodiversity, and technology galleries.
  • 3D Theatre & Planetarium: Immersive experiences with advanced projection systems.
  • Science Park: Outdoor installations including a Dinosaur Park.
  • Workshops & Demonstrations: Hands-on activities for all ages.
  • Facilities: Library, auditorium, conference hall, and computer lab.

About Pilikula Regional Science Centre

Pilikula Regional Science Centre (PRSC) is a public science museum located in Mangaluru, Karnataka, India. It is part of the larger Pilikula Nisargadhama, a multi-purpose eco-education and tourism complex managed by the District Administration of Dakshina Kannada.

Location

PRSC is situated at Moodushedde, about 9 km from Mangaluru city, accessible via road, rail, and air. It is nestled within a 10-acre campus surrounded by lush greenery.

Establishment

The centre was developed jointly by:

  • Department of Science & Technology, Government of Karnataka
  • National Council of Science Museums (NCSM), Ministry of Culture, Government of India

It was inaugurated in 2014 as part of a national initiative to promote science education and public engagement.

Facilities and Exhibits

  • Science Galleries: Fun Science, Biodiversity, and Technology
  • 3D Theatre: Immersive science films
  • Planetarium: India’s first 8K hybrid projection planetarium
  • Science Park: Outdoor installations including a Dinosaur Park
  • Innovation Hub: Hands-on science and creativity space
  • Library and Auditorium: For lectures and workshops
  • Chemistry Lab: For experiments and demonstrations

Educational Role

PRSC conducts regular science demonstrations, teacher training programs, student workshops, and mobile science exhibitions. It plays a vital role in STEM outreach across the region.

Integration with Pilikula Nisargadhama

PRSC is part of the broader Pilikula Nisargadhama, which includes:

  • Biological park
  • Botanical garden and arboretum
  • Heritage village
  • Lake and boating facilities
